---
title: 'Learner Profiles'
---

This lesson targets members of the ePIC collaboration — students, postdocs, and researchers — who
need to find and access EIC simulation campaign data for their analysis or detector/reconstruction
work. Learners are expected to have a working `eic-shell` environment and some familiarity with the
UNIX command line and CERN ROOT and/or Python. No prior experience with Rucio or xrootd is assumed.
